## Session ending

They stop at about 21 minutes because they have run out of new things: by 14:00 they had touched everything on the map, and the self-made "first to five" duel carried the last six minutes. There is no round, win screen, shop or unlock to give them a reason to stay, and Robin says "bored" first. They leave together for a bigger fighting game they already play. Kai suggests coming back "to defend the records" only if Sam joins; Robin says "only for the bench".
