## How it ended

They quit at 16:40 because they ran out of things to find: every spot on the map had been visited by about minute 10, and the fighting, the only thing with any push-back, had turned into Kai spawn-killing Robin with no score to make it matter and no sound to make it feel like anything. Nobody rage-quit; Kai said "there's nothing to DO" and Robin agreed. They stayed on the same Discord call and joined an obby together, with Kai still claiming a 6-1 win that the game never recorded.
