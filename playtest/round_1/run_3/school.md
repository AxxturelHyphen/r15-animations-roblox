# Round 1, run 3: next day, lunch table

Sam: What were you two doing last night? You were both in voice for ages.

Kai: My cousin sent me this Roblox fighting thing. He said the animations were good.

Robin: It's not a game. Tell Sam it's not a game.

Kai: It's not a game. It's a test place. I told my cousin that.

Sam: What do you mean it's not a game?

Robin: You spawn on the baseplate. Like the actual grey studio baseplate. No menu, no lobby, nothing.

Kai: I thought it was still loading. It was not loading.

Sam: So what do you even do?

Kai: You fight. There's punches, and if you mash you get a combo, and there's a sword.

Robin: The sword is so broken. It has this lunge so Kai just flies at you from miles away.

Kai: The lunge is clean though. The fighting LOOKS really good, I'll give it that.

Robin: OK but the best part. When you die, you don't explode or anything. Your guy falls over, rolls onto his back and just lies there.

Kai: Like he's going to bed.

Robin: I took a NAP. In the middle of a sword fight. I was crying.

Kai: Robin kept going "nap time, go to sleep" every single time they got me.

Sam: Ha. OK that's kind of funny.

Robin: And there's no sound. At all. So you just silently lie down.

Kai: Yeah there's zero sound when you hit someone. You can't tell if it hit unless you stare at the health bar.

Robin: We did a first to five with swords. We had to count ourselves in. Like out loud. "Three, two, one."

Kai: And I won.

Robin: You did NOT win. You spawn killed me! I was still getting my sword out!

Kai: There's no rules! It was two-zero.

Robin: Then Kai said it was three-two and it was three-three.

Kai: It was not three-three, that one was before we started.

Sam: Doesn't the game say the score?

Kai: NO. That's the whole thing. There's no kill counter. No leaderboard. It doesn't say who won. Nothing.

Robin: We literally argued about the score for like half the time.

Kai: I got to four and Robin just started running away.

Robin: Because according to who! Nobody was counting!

Sam: Is there anything else? Like a map?

Robin: There's a dummy. Just a guy standing there breathing at you. I said hello sir.

Kai: We jumped him. And when he dies he lies down too, and then a new one just pops back in the same spot.

Robin: "HE'S BACK." Right in our faces.

Kai: It takes seven sword hits. I counted. And you get nothing for it.

Robin: Nerd. Oh, and you can type slash e dance and you dance. In silence. No music.

Kai: And I killed Robin while they were dancing.

Robin: You can't kill a man while he's dancing! I went from dancing straight to napping.

Kai: That was the funniest one.

Sam: What about the rest of the map?

Kai: There's a tower you climb and there's nothing on top. I thought there'd be a chest.

Robin: We jumped off it though. Your arms go all flaily. And then you land and you're just fine. No fall damage.

Kai: Then we ran to the edge to find more map and it's just the void. That's the whole game.

Robin: Oh, and there's a block button, and I was invincible.

Kai: That was so boring. You just stood there.

Sam: So should I get it?

Kai: No. It's mid. There's nothing to do. No rounds, no kills, no coins.

Robin: We got bored in like fifteen minutes and went on the battlegrounds one instead.

Kai: The one that actually counts kills.

Sam: Would you play it again?

Kai: Nah. Not unless they add a kill counter and sound. Then maybe, because the sword fights were actually good.

Robin: Same. If hits made a noise it'd be so good. Right now it's just the nap.

Kai: The nap was funny though.

Robin: The nap was SO funny.
