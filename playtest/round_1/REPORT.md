# Simulated playtest — round 1

3 independent simulated sessions of the same two friends (see `playtest/personas.md`). A moment is **talked about** if they brought it up at school in at least 2 of the runs, and **forgotten** if it happened but came up in at most 1. A moment mentioned mostly negatively is **complained about**: memorable for the wrong reason. This is an LLM simulation grounded in the code, not a real playtest.

## Why (from the next-day recall)

- **M01** (complained about): Spawning itself was nothing, but getting spawn camped before the sword was out stuck with Robin as unfair. / Only survives as 'you spawn and it's just a grey floor'; nothing happened there. / Spawning on a bare grey baseplate is the first thing they tell Sam, as proof it is not a real game.
- **M02** (forgotten): Running around was just how you got places. / Running around was fine and felt like nothing. / The run looked clean in the moment but nothing happened with it, so it faded.
- **M03** (forgotten): A few jumps at spawn that led to nothing. / Bunny-hopping between things left no trace.
- **M04** (forgotten): Crouching was slow and there was nobody to hide from. / Five seconds of 'we're ninjas' with nothing to sneak up on; gone by morning. / Crouch was declared useless in ten seconds and never came up again.
- **M05** (talked about): Kai remembers winning the race up, but mostly as the way to the jump, and the top had nothing. / The truss is only remembered as the tower you dance off; the made-up race and the empty top are forgotten. / Remembered only as the tower with nothing on top where Kai wanted a chest.
- **M06** (talked about): Flailing arms on a counted-down jump together was the biggest shared laugh. / Dancing off the edge with flailing arms was the biggest shared laugh, though it wore out after a few repeats. / The flailing arms were funny, but landing with no fall damage made it a one-time joke.
- **M07** (talked about): Remembered only as 'the pool's nothing'; the swim animation itself is forgotten. / Remembered as a random pool with nothing at the bottom; 'mid, it's just water'. / The pool was pleasant, had nothing at the bottom, and left no story to tell.
- **M08** (forgotten): Sitting on a bench was a few seconds of goofing with no payoff. / Sitting on the bench and getting killed on it felt like nothing even at the time. / Sitting on the bench led to six seconds of silence and an 'ok'.
- **M10** (forgotten): Punching air was instantly overwritten by punching Robin. / Shadow-boxing the air only fed the general 'game is on mute' impression. / Punching air was just the first button press, overwritten by the sword.
- **M11** (forgotten): Hitting the dummy gave no feedback, so only its pointless death is recalled. / First silent punch on the dummy was overwritten by the sword. / Punching the dummy blurred into the general memory of jumping it.
- **M12** (forgotten): The punch combo was exciting for a minute and then the sword replaced it. / The combo was a small spark that nothing built on. / Kai recalls that mashing gives a combo and that the fighting looks good, without specifics.
- **M13** (forgotten): Kick was one more button found by mashing and never used again after the sword. / Finding the kick was pleasant for a second and never mattered again. / The kick was a brief discovery that the sword immediately made irrelevant.
- **M14** (talked about): Holding F is remembered only as the setup for the 'blocking is broken' argument. / Learning the block key is forgotten; only what blocking did to the fight stuck. / Block survives only as 'there's a block button', since they never could tell if it worked.
- **M15** (talked about): Pressing 1 and a sword appearing was Kai's biggest surprise of the night. / 'You have a sword' is how they describe the game, but the equip moment itself is not retold. / Pressing 1 to equip is plumbing; they remember the sword, not drawing it.
- **M16** (talked about): Sword does huge damage and was how every kill happened, though they remember it being silent. / Both agree the slash looks sick; it's the one thing they praise about the game. / The sword lunge is what made the fights good and what Robin calls broken.
- **M17** (complained about): Dummy gives nothing and just comes back, so it's one dismissive line. / The dummy lying down then reappearing and giving nothing became the 'immortal guy' joke and a complaint. / The dummy lying down and popping back in their faces was a surprise, and Kai still knows it takes seven hits for nothing.
- **M18** (talked about): The first punch making a health bar appear was the moment the game started. / Hitting a friend working is the premise of the retelling, but the mushy trading itself blurred together. / The first-to-five with a self-counted countdown and the spawn-kill argument is their main shared story.
- **M19** (talked about): The roll-over death made Robin laugh every time and is the score Kai is still arguing about. / The bet fight where the loser had to say the other is better, plus the disputed 7-2 score, is the headline story. / The roll-over-and-nap death is the biggest laugh of the night and the only thing both call funny.
- **M20** (complained about): Hitting a blocker looked like lag; it's remembered as broken, and funny only because Robin lied about it. / Blocking looked like lag with no feedback and killed the fight; Kai is still annoyed. / Robin remembers being invincible; Kai remembers it as boring to hit a silent wall.
- **M30** (forgotten): Waving at a dummy that ignores you was a tiny giggle that didn't last. / Waving hello and goodbye was sweet but too small to survive. / Waving at the dummy got no reaction, so there was nothing to remember.
- **M31** (talked about): Dancing alone in a silent 'parking lot' was a funny image Robin made into a story. / 'Dancing to nothing' is remembered only as the setup for the jump; the failed synced dance party is forgotten. / Dancing in total silence was a quick giggle that mostly sticks because of what happened next.
- **M32** (forgotten): Salute, laugh, cheer, clap and point each played once and felt like ticking boxes. / The silent laugh over Kai's body was cursed and became the running gag. / Working through the emote list was Robin ticking boxes while Kai waited.
- **M33** (forgotten): No dab and silently failing emotes made it seem like chat was broken. / Guessed emotes being swallowed was a string of small letdowns, not a story. / Emotes that did nothing were a small confusion that got dropped.
- **M34** (forgotten): The dance cutting off when hit wasn't noticed as its own thing. / Dance cancelling on movement was a minor annoyance that didn't stick. / Being slashed mid-dance and going straight from dancing to napping got a real laugh from both.
- **M40** (talked about): The dummy just standing there breathing is forgotten; only that you can beat it up remains. / The dummy 'just stands there staring at you' gets one line as part of the immortal-guy bit. / Robin remembers saying 'hello sir' to the creepy breathing guy before they realised it was a dummy.
- **M41** (complained about): Running for a minute into endless grey was the most boring part and sticks as proof the game is empty. / The absurdly long run to the edge of the world is remembered for the commitment, not the destination. / Running to the void and realising the baseplate was the whole game was the big letdown.
- **M42** (complained about): No score, menu or goal is the main reason they quit and why Kai says no for now. / No rounds, leaderboard, ranks or shop is the reason they give for not playing again. / No kill counter, score, sound or goal is the complaint they repeat most and the reason they quit.

## Would they play again

run 1: **maybe**, run 2: **no**, run 3: **no**

- Run 1 pitch to Sam: "It's a grey parking lot where you find a sword by mashing keys, kill your friend so they do this hilarious roll-over death, and jump off a tower flapping your arms, but it's silent and there's nothing else to do."
- Run 2 pitch to Sam: "It's a grey test map where the sword looks sick and you can dance off a tower, but there's no actual game and nothing keeps score."
- Run 3 pitch to Sam: "It's not a game, it's a test place on a baseplate: the sword fights look good and you take a nap when you die, but there's no sound, no kill counter and nothing to do."
